Version: V56

## Product thesis

AE Brawl is a cinematic fight-discovery experience. It turns local movement, combat-sport curiosity, gym discovery, and first-night storytelling into a guided product flow.

The experience is designed to feel like Bruce Lee discipline mixed with Double Dragon pressure: sharp, physical, readable, and alive.

## What it is

- A human-readable site/app experience for discovering fight-adjacent training signals.
- A cinematic interface layer for choosing a discipline, reviewing a venue-style dossier, and leaving with a safe story.
- A demonstration of Codex-assisted product design, frontend assembly, UX copy, safety boundary writing, and submission packaging.

## What it is not

- Not an unsupervised fight-matching service.
- Not a promise of real venue participation unless a venue relationship is explicitly live.
- Not a gambling, betting, or combat wagering system.
- Not a system for harassment, intimidation, or non-consensual contact.

## Lead MCP explanation

Lead MCP is the routing idea underneath the presentation:

1. A visitor arrives with vague intent.
2. The interface turns that intent into a structured signal.
3. The system separates real data, fictional framing, safety rules, and next action.
4. The product returns a route a person can understand and a judge can test.

For this submission, Lead MCP is presented as a judge-readable product pattern: capture intent, classify the lead, preserve boundaries, and produce a safe next step.

## Innovation claims

1. Cinematic discovery interface  
   The page uses emotional staging, short rounds, readable controls, and clear action hierarchy instead of a generic directory.

2. Fiction/real boundary discipline  
   Fictional codenames and mythology remain visibly separate from real venue facts.

3. Consent-first combat UX  
   The product can feel intense without encouraging unsafe or non-consensual behavior.

4. Submission packet design  
   The site page doubles as a Devpost field source, video script, and judge orientation room.

5. Codex-native production workflow  
   Codex is used as builder, editor, QA assistant, and packaging operator rather than a prompt-only copy generator.

## Safety boundary

AE Brawl should only route people toward consensual, supervised, lawful movement and training. Any real-world gym, venue, or event integration requires venue approval, insurance/safety planning, trained supervision, and clear participant consent.
