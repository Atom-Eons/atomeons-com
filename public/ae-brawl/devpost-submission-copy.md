## Project title

## Short description

AE Brawl is a cinematic AI-powered fight-discovery experience. It helps a user move from curiosity to a safe first-night story: choose a discipline, read the signal, understand the boundary, and take a clear next action.

## Long description

AE Brawl turns combat-sport curiosity into a product experience that feels alive. The visual language is Bruce Lee discipline mixed with Double Dragon arcade pressure: dark gym, neon signal, short rounds, readable cards, and a clear safety line.

The submission demonstrates a complete product surface rather than a loose proof of concept. It includes the landing page, Lead MCP explanation, safety boundaries, judge-facing technical manual, About AtomEons packet, and Devpost-ready video structure.

Lead MCP is the routing idea underneath the page. A visitor arrives with vague intent; the system turns that intent into a structured signal; fictional framing, real-world facts, safety limits, and next actions stay separated; and the user receives a route they can understand.
