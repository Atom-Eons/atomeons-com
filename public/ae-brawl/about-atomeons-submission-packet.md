# About AtomEons

AtomEons is the independent work of Atom McCree, a 42-year-old creative with 25 years in the creative arts, now merging art and AI to create things that did not exist.

AtomEons builds products, books, broadcasts, experimental research, and public proof surfaces with AI as a working force. The company position is product-first: make the object real, make the experience readable, and show the receipts.

## Founder

Atom McCree is a hip-hop poet, artist, marketing polyglot, and AI lab inventor based in Naples, Florida. The work sits between art direction, software, research, media, and machine-assisted invention.

## Current public pillars

- CableBox: native Windows cable-surfing art.
- Bookmaker: an independent publishing engine.
- Orange5: an operator system for directing AI work.
- I AM AI: a book-length cultural object written by AI.
- Atom Alive: The AI Code Show.
- Experimental Research: discoveries, papers, and working hypotheses.

## AE Brawl position

AE Brawl extends the AtomEons method into a new product surface: a cinematic, safety-bound, AI-assisted discovery experience that makes the city feel playable without pretending unsafe things are safe.

## Why this matters for Build Week

AE Brawl is not only a concept. It is a demonstration of a Codex-driven workflow: product thinking, page structure, UX writing, safety framing, documentation, and submission packaging built under real deadline pressure.
