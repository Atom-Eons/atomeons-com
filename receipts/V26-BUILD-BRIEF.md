# AtomEons V26 build brief

## Goal

Make atomeons.com a two-object public launch system. CableBox 2 is live, free, open source, and starts with the browser edition. Atomic Orange is the incoming second object and must be described without implying a public release that does not exist yet. Everything else remains available as research and archive material.

## Public hierarchy

1. CableBox 2: try the web edition, then download the verified Windows release.
2. Atomic Orange: understand the promise and follow the build signal.
3. Research: preserve papers, discoveries, books, broadcasts, and prior creations behind one calm gateway.
4. About: the human story, press, contact, and GitHub organization.

## Acceptance gates

- The first viewport explains CableBox 2 and offers a working Try now action.
- Free and open source are visible before technical detail.
- The Windows download resolves to the exact organization-owned GitHub release.
- Atomic Orange is prominent but never presented as downloadable before proof exists.
- The primary navigation has no more than five choices.
- The visual field remains useful without WebGL and honors reduced motion.
- Desktop and phone layouts preserve readable type, touch targets, and visible actions.
- `/brawl` remains byte-identical to hosted version 25.
- Existing routes and research resources are preserved.
- Build, route, asset, checksum, browser, and live production probes pass.

## No-go boundaries

- Do not edit AE Brawl.
- Do not delete legacy products, papers, books, or research routes.
- Do not publish Atomic Orange source, internals, or a download without a verified handoff.
- Do not reuse the old CableBox 1 release or personal-account URL as the new canonical download.
- Do not add tracking, fake metrics, fake institutional language, or a technical machine rail to the front door.
